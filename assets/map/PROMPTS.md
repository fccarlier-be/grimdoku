# Carte de la baronnie de Brumeval — prompt de génération

À terme, la carte remplace le classement par taille (décision de François, 2026-10-17) : chaque lieu y est un endroit
où l'on clique pour voir ses enquêtes. Fichier attendu : `assets/map/brumeval.png` (ou `.jpeg`), paysage 3:2,
au moins 2400 px de large, **sans aucun texte** (les noms des lieux sont posés par l'appli, en HTML).

```
A hand-drawn fantasy map of a small humorous barony, seen from above at a slight angle, Franco-Belgian comic book style (bande dessinée), bold black ink outlines, flat muted earthy colors (parchment, ochre, moss green, slate blue, brick red), drawn on an old parchment sheet with burnt torn edges.
Landscape 3:2. No text, no labels, no letters, no compass rose letters, no legend, no watermark.
Layout, left to right:
- WEST, a misty coast: a smugglers' harbour with a lighthouse and wooden docks, a large harbour town, a ghostly ship anchored in the fog offshore, and steaming thermal baths in stone arches on a cliff.
- CENTER, the countryside: a crossroads with a half-timbered tavern (a one-eyed boar on its sign), a crooked windmill, a haunted farm with a scarecrow, and a foggy swamp with a stilt hut and reeds.
- NORTH-CENTER, the capital on a hill: a dark gothic castle with a fortified citadel wall around it, a moonlit cemetery with a small chapel beside it, and a tall slender wizard's tower with a glowing observatory.
- EAST, the Grimace Mountains: grumpy-looking peaks, a dwarven mine entrance with rails, a smoking forge built into the rock with a sleeping red dragon curled on top, and a goblin banquet hall dug into the mountainside with lanterns.
- SOUTH-EAST, the scholars' quarter: a magic academy with towers and a greenhouse, a larger university with a domed amphitheatre, and a dark forbidden library.
- SOUTH, near the road: an empty meadow with tent pegs where a travelling fair sets up (a few colourful tents and a Ferris wheel), and at the far south edge an exotic domed palace with lanterns, as if it belonged to a faraway land.
Small winding roads link every place. Leave some calm empty space around each place so labels can be added on top later.
```
